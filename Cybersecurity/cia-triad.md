\# CIA Triad - Cybersecurity Fundamentals



\## Introduction



The CIA Triad is a fundamental model in cybersecurity.

It consists of three main principles:



1\. Confidentiality

2\. Integrity

3\. Availability



\## 1. Confidentiality



Confidentiality ensures that sensitive information

is accessible only to authorized users.



Example:

\- Password protection

\- Data encryption

\- Multi-factor authentication (MFA)



\## 2. Integrity



Integrity ensures that information remains accurate,

consistent, and protected from unauthorized modification.



Example:

\- File hashing

\- Digital signatures

\- Access controls



\## 3. Availability



Availability ensures that systems, applications,

and information remain accessible when needed.



Example:

\- Regular backups

\- Disaster recovery

\- Protection against DDoS attacks



\## Real-World Example



Consider an online banking application:



\- Confidentiality: Only authorized users can access accounts.

\- Integrity: Nobody can secretly modify account balances.

\- Availability: Customers can access banking services when needed.



\## Key Takeaway



The CIA Triad helps organizations protect information

and build secure systems.



All three principles are essential for effective cybersecurity.
